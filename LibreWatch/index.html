LibreWatch

Glucose on your wrist.

LibreWatch is a standalone Apple Watch viewer for your LibreLinkUp readings. Sign in once, pick a complication, and your latest value is always a glance away.

Getting started

  1. Make sure a friend or family member is sharing data with you in the official LibreLinkUp iPhone app, or that you have your own active connection.
  2. Open LibreWatch on your Apple Watch and tap the gear icon.
  3. Enter your LibreLinkUp email, password, and region, then tap Connect.
  4. Long-press your watch face → Edit → Complications → place LibreWatch in any slot.

FAQ

How often does the value refresh?

The app requests a refresh every 5 minutes in the background. watchOS may delay individual refreshes to conserve battery — refreshes are most reliable when the LibreWatch complication is on the watch face you're currently using.

The app says "no connections" after I sign in.

Your LibreLinkUp account exists but no one is sharing glucose data with it yet. Open the official LibreLinkUp iPhone app and confirm at least one active connection is listed under People I Follow.

I got "wrong region" or the app keeps redirecting.

LibreLinkUp routes accounts to regional servers. In Settings, change the Region picker to match where the account was created (most commonly United States, Europe, or Canada).

Will my readings show up in Apple Health?

Not at this time. LibreWatch is a viewer only.
